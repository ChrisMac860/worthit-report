Worth It? Support

Worth It? is a session-based drink logger for supported Garmin watches. It records sessions on the watch and can optionally open a private, browser-based report on the paired phone.

Quick checks

  • No heart-rate data: confirm the SensorHistory permission is enabled and allow enough time for the watch to record samples.
  • Could not reach phone: confirm the watch is paired and connected to Garmin Connect Mobile over Bluetooth, then try Send to phone again.
  • Report will not open: confirm the phone has internet access to load the static page. Session data itself remains in the URL fragment.
  • Delete everything: choose Menu → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app.

Report-page test

Open the built-in report self-test. It should display ALL TESTS PASSED.

Contact and bug reports

Open an issue in the public Worth It? support tracker. Include your Garmin model, app version shown in the Connect IQ Store, what you expected, and what happened. Do not include private health data or a report URL containing a session fragment.
