Worth It? Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026

Worth It? is a Garmin Connect IQ watch app that records drinking sessions and available heart-rate context. The app is designed to keep this sensitive personal data on your watch.

What the app stores

The app stores session start and end times, drink timestamps, optional heart-rate statistics and trace, optional temperature readings, your Famous Last Words response, and app settings in Garmin Connect IQ application storage on your watch. Storage is capped at 60 sessions; older sessions are removed first.

What leaves the watch

Nothing leaves the watch automatically. The app has no account, backend, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or automatic upload.

If you explicitly choose Send to phone, the selected session and a small number of previous summaries are encoded after the # in a URL and opened in your paired phone's browser. Browsers do not send URL fragments to web servers. The static report page processes the fragment locally in the browser and makes no external requests when it loads.

The report page includes optional links to this policy, support, and Buy Me a Coffee. Those destinations are contacted only if you tap their links. The Share button creates an image locally and invokes your device's share controls; the report page does not upload the image.

Permissions

  • SensorHistory reads heart-rate history, and temperature history where available, when a session ends.
  • Communications opens the optional report page on the paired phone when you choose Send to phone.

The app does not request GPS/location, background, advertising, or general web-request permissions.

Retention and deletion

Use Menu → Delete all data to remove the app's sessions and settings from the watch. Uninstalling the app also removes its application storage. No copy is held on an application server because the developer does not receive the data.

Garmin

Garmin may independently process data collected by your Garmin device under Garmin's own privacy terms. Worth It? does not send drink logs or session records to Garmin Connect and does not create Garmin activities or FIT files.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before any future release changes how data is collected, used, stored, or disclosed. For questions or support, use the Worth It? support page.
